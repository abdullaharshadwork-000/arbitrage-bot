## Risk disclosure

ArbiCore automates configured trading instructions. It does not provide a
guarantee of profit. Digital-asset trading can lose some or all deposited
capital. Prices, liquidity, fees, exchange availability, API latency, partial
fills, and regulatory requirements can change without notice. Paper and
Testnet results do not prove that a strategy will perform the same way with
real funds.

Users remain responsible for exchange permissions, tax and legal obligations,
strategy selection, risk limits, and supervising open positions. Withdrawal
permissions must remain disabled on every exchange API key.

## Privacy notice

The application stores account identity, hashed passwords, preferences,
versioned consent records, configurations, audit events, trades, order intents,
and balance snapshots. API secrets are persisted only when an operator supplies
`ARBICORE_MASTER_KEY`; persisted secrets are encrypted. Never place plaintext
credentials in logs, screenshots, support messages, source control, or browser
storage.

Before public launch, qualified counsel must replace this placeholder with
jurisdiction-specific Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, cookie/session notice,
financial-risk disclosure, eligibility rules, and retention/deletion policy.
